# A5 simplex family projections - viewer index

**[Open this page on GitHub Pages](https://nanma80.github.io/zomeable-4polytopes/output/a5_projections/VIEWER.html)** for the viewer index.

Strict orthographic zomeable projections of the A5 (5-simplex) uniform family.  The polytope-independent raw-column `Z[phi]^3` sweep saturates at three distinct projection geometries, applied to each of the three rectifications below.

"Balls" counts distinct 3D ball positions in the projected vZome model; some vertices of the source polytope may coincide in 3D.
